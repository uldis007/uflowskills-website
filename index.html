About UFlow Skills

UFlow Skills is an independent publishing brand. Its books are published under the imprint UFlow Press.

The name joins U with flow: skills that move easily from the page into everyday life. The logo pairs a horseshoe, a sign of good luck and a firm foundation, with two waves that stand for growth and abundance.
